## They Don't Know Why

Most of the time they don't know the reason from either

1. Benefits to the team/org for doing it or harm for not doing it
 - Example: Putting points on jira tickets is a waste of time. 
 - Reason: Putting points on jira allows for the team to normalize work and perform proper accounting.

2. Benefits to employee or harm for not doing it
 - benefit: Performance Rating, paid, raise, award, promotion, choice assignment, award, recognition
 - harm: avoid embarrassment, prevent pay from stopping, reprimand, demotion, termination

### Preventative

Follow this 
1. Before work starts, explain in depts the benefit/harm to the org.  Describe the relationship between the task and other departments/customers and mission.  Don't rush it.
2. To have them change for the purpose or improve quality
 - Explain the problem in detail
 - Explain the goal in detail
 - Discuss the solution in detail
 - Explain the expected beenfit of success
 - Explain the expected agony of defeat
3. definitely do 2 when the task is undesirable or difficult.  Think of a sales person being asked to make notes after calls.  This helps the person and also the org for if someone else has to take over the account. 
4. Don't focus on honor and glory of company, boil it to real benefits
5. Explain the second category of reasons (benefits or harm to them) in detail.  The negative fulfills legal need for if you terminate.  They knew.
